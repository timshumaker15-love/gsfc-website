Private Pilot License

The foundation certificate every pilot starts with — what it lets you do, and what it costs at Golden State.

A Private Pilot License lets you fly a single-engine airplane under Visual Flight Rules, by day or night, with passengers aboard — the certificate every pilot earns first, whether flying is the destination or the first step toward a career.

Training combines flight time with your instructor and solo practice, ground instruction covering regulations and navigation, and an optional in-person ground school to prepare for the FAA Knowledge Test. See Getting Your License for the full step-by-step process and The 4 Stages of Training for how flight lessons themselves are structured.

At a Glance

  • FAA minimum 35 hours — most students take 55–70
  • 20 hours dual flight instruction, 10 hours ground (FAA minimums)
  • Optional 11-week in-person ground school
  • FAA Knowledge Test, then a checkride with an FAA examiner

Costs can be paid by the hour, or at a discount for block time of $1,500 or more. Flying regularly helps reduce total hours needed.
