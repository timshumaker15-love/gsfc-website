Free Saturday Morning Orientation Class

For anyone curious about flying — no obligation, just call ahead to reserve a seat.

Most Saturday mornings, Golden State holds a 2.5-hour presentation (9–11:30 AM) for anyone who has purchased, is considering, or received as a gift our Introductory Flight Special — two one-hour flights, two courtesy nights sitting in on ground school, an FAA textbook, and an official logbook.

The goal is to build familiarity with how airplanes fly and work before your first flight, so you can really learn while you're in the air — especially since you'll be at the controls for more than half of that first flight.

These sessions are popular. You're welcome to sit in with no obligation — call (619) 449-0611 to reserve a seat, or sign up using the form.

Golden State's Famous Introductory Course

Choosing a flight school? Take the intro flight first.

Think you might be interested in learning to fly, but haven't decided for sure? Or already decided, but not sure where to train? Narrow your choices to 2–3 schools based on research, recommendations, and a phone call or visit. Location matters, but it's often worth driving a few extra miles to the school that fits you best. Then look closely at how each school operates, get to know an instructor or two, and get a feel for the equipment — the best way to do all of this is to take an Introductory Flight at each school.

We never fly with a student before a thorough briefing about the flight ahead — what to expect, what you'll learn, and what to watch for. For a routine lesson, that briefing happens one-on-one; for the Introductory Course, we do it in a classroom format most Saturday mornings, 9–11:30 AM (call ahead during the week to confirm). If you've purchased a course or received one as a gift, call to get on the list for the next Orientation Class — you can often schedule a flight immediately afterward if you call a few days in advance. We also cover FAA licensing requirements, costs, and time involved in detail. Even if you're just curious, you're welcome to attend any Saturday by calling ahead to reserve a seat.
