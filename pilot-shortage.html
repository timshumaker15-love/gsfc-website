Take Advantage of the Pilot Shortage

With a national pilot shortage and rising demand for air travel, there's rarely been a better time to start toward a professional flying career.

As you may have heard, pilots are in high demand across the industry, with a shortage both in the short term and looming over the industry for decades to come. An aging workforce, FAA-mandated retirements, and continually growing demand for air travel are worrying the airlines — but creating a very positive market for pilots, with rising salaries and available bonuses.

If you're considering a career as a professional airline pilot, there has rarely been a better time to start down this path and benefit from it for years to come.
