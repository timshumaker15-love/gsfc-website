Private Pilot Certificate

What it takes to earn your license

Making the decision to become a pilot is exciting, and when you complete your Private Pilot training at Golden State you'll be confident in your abilities. A Private Pilot Certificate lets you fly in Visual Flight Rule (VFR) conditions — perfect for sunny San Diego weather.

RequirementFAA MinimumTypical at Golden State
Aircraft Rental35 hours55–70 hours (most students)
Dual Flight Instruction20 hoursVaries by student
Ground Instruction (one-on-one)10 hoursVaries by student
Private Pilot Ground School (classroom)Optional — ask us about other options

Course materials included: Private Pilot course syllabus, Private Pilot textbook, ASA Private Pilot written test prep guide, aluminum E-6B flight computer, flexible plastic navigational plotter, Piper Warrior pilot's information manual, private pilot oral exam guide, and the Private Pilot Airman Certification Standards booklet. Sales tax applies to books and supplies.

Pay As You Go

Fly at your own pace

Start with the $199.95 Introductory Flight Special — two one-hour flights and instruction. From there, most students continue with weekly or bi-weekly lessons, paying as they go or putting money into their flying club account ahead of time.

Purchasing block time in increments up to $1,500 earns a 5% discount on remaining costs. Fly as often as you like — just check the flight schedule and write yourself in next to your instructor's name.
