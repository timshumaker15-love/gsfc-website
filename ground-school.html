Private Pilot Ground School

An 11-week, in-person classroom course preparing new and current student pilots for the FAA Private Pilot Knowledge Test.

Structured, in-person classroom instruction at Gillespie Field for new and current student pilots preparing for private pilot training and the FAA Private Pilot Knowledge Test.

This 11-week course is built for students who want structure, live instruction, and help preparing for the FAA Knowledge Test — not a self-paced online video series.

ScheduleTuesdays & Thursdays, 6:30–9:00 PM
Length11 weeks
LocationGillespie Field
Tuition$455 + materials

Golden State runs two sessions a year — a Summer session (Jul–Sep) and a Fall session (Oct–Dec). The next session starts October 6, 2026. Enroll and pay before the start date for 10% off tuition.

Why Take It In Person?

Online ground school can work well for students who want a cheaper, faster, self-paced course. Golden State's in-person course is for students who want structure, live instruction, accountability, and the ability to ask questions in a real classroom.

  • Structure: a set class schedule every Tuesday and Thursday keeps you consistent.
  • Questions: ask a qualified instructor in person about airspace, weather, navigation, and regulations.
  • Accountability: a real reason to show up and stay on track.
  • Flight training: ground school knowledge makes your flight lessons make more sense.
  • Local support: learn alongside other student pilots and instructors at Gillespie Field.
Curriculum

What you'll learn

Golden State's Private Pilot Ground School covers the major knowledge areas students need for private pilot training and the FAA Private Pilot Knowledge Test.

Basic Aerodynamics Aircraft Systems Aircraft Performance Weight & Balance Aeronautical Charts Airport Operations Radio Communications FAA Regulations Flight Computer Use Weather for Pilots Weather Services & Charts Radio Navigation Cross-Country Planning Aeromedical Factors SRM & ADM Final Exam Review

This course helps students prepare for the FAA Private Pilot Knowledge Test. Passing results are not guaranteed.

Materials & Testing

Golden State uses the Jeppesen Private Pilot textbook as its main reference. Students also need a sectional chart, an E6B flight computer, and a plotter — sold separately from tuition. Contact us before class begins for the current materials list and pricing.

After preparation, the FAA Private Pilot Knowledge Test can be taken on-site at Golden State's C.A.T.S. testing computer — no need to find a separate testing location. The FAA computerized knowledge test fee is currently listed at $175, subject to change and separate from tuition and materials. Results are received immediately, and a passing score is valid for 24 calendar months.

Course Value

Tuition is $455 plus required materials. Enroll and pay before the session start date for 10% off tuition (materials and testing fees are not discounted). Students who've paid tuition may also be able to sit in on a future session for review at no extra charge if they need a refresher.
