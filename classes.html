Classes for Pilots and Student Pilots

Golden State has offered primary and advanced ground school classes at Gillespie Field for over five decades.

Our classes are taught in a classroom setting by experienced flight and ground instructors and include lectures, workshops, and stage tests. We're also an FAA-authorized Testing Center, so graduates can take the FAA Knowledge Test right here after completing a ground course.

At Golden State, learning to fly is about more than passing tests and mastering basic flying skills. Our instructors take the time to make sure you have a complete understanding of both the knowledge and practical sides of becoming a pilot. Enroll and pay tuition and books before the start date to get 10% off.
