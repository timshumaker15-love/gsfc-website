Meet the Golden State Team

The owners, instructors, and staff who've been putting students in the left seat at Gillespie Field for decades.

Golden State Flying Club is family-owned, and it shows in how long our owners, instructors, and staff stick around. Here's who you'll likely meet on your way to a certificate — on the ramp, in the classroom, or behind the front desk.

Ownership

Owner

Owner

Has run Golden State Flying Club at Gillespie Field, overseeing the fleet, the club's members, and its instructors.

CO

Co-Owner

Co-Owner

Shares in the day-to-day operation of the club, from scheduling to member relations.

Management

GM

General Manager

General Manager

Oversees daily club operations, aircraft maintenance scheduling, and member accounts.

Flight Instructors

CFI

Chief Flight Instructor

Chief Flight Instructor

Oversees the training program and stage checks, and flies with students throughout every phase of Private Pilot, Instrument, and Commercial training.

FI

Flight Instructor

Flight Instructor

Teaches day-to-day flight lessons from first solo through checkride prep, and leads sessions of the Private Pilot Ground School.

FI

Flight Instructor

Flight Instructor (CFII)

Specializes in Instrument Rating training and IFR proficiency flights for current club members.

Finance & Administration

FD

Finance Director

Finance Director

Oversees club billing, member accounts, financing partnerships, and aircraft cost accounting.

Office & Front Desk

OM

Office Manager

Office Manager / Scheduling

Runs the front desk, manages the flight schedule and billing, and is usually the first friendly voice you'll reach by phone.

FD

Front Desk

Office Staff

Helps members and students book aircraft, handles walk-ins, and keeps the clubhouse running smoothly.
