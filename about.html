About Golden State Flying Club

A family-owned flight school and flying club at Gillespie Field, training San Diego County pilots since 1968.

Our Story

Nearly six decades of flight training at Gillespie Field

Golden State Flying Club has trained pilots at Gillespie Field in El Cajon since 1968. We're an FAA Part 141 approved flight school with our own training curriculum, classroom classes, and certified flight instructors — and, as a flying club, a fleet of aircraft available for training and member trips alike.

Unlike a school you graduate from and leave, Golden State is built to keep you flying. Earn your Private Pilot certificate, and you can continue training toward an Instrument Rating or Commercial Certificate, or simply keep flying the airplanes you already know as a club member.

We're also an FAA/PSI Authorized Testing Center, so students can take their FAA Knowledge Test right here after finishing a ground course — no need to travel to a separate testing site.
