Why Golden State

A Flight School AND Flying Club

Since we're a club as well as a school, you can keep flying the airplanes you already know, keep building hours, and keep progressing toward advanced ratings, right here.

Pay As You Go

Start with the Intro Flight Special, then pay for lessons weekly, bi-weekly, or in block-time increments — block purchases of $1,500 earn a 5% discount.

Your Own Pace

Most students finish their Private Pilot certificate in about five months, though some finish in less than half that time. Train on your schedule, not ours.

Club Membership

Join for $35/month per family and get free use of the clubhouse, headsets, simulator, Wi-Fi, and more — plus club rates on every aircraft.

Getting Started

Your first flight, step by step

1

Free Orientation

Attend a no-cost 2.5-hour Saturday orientation class covering everything you need to know to decide if flying is for you.

2

Meet the Fleet

Tour the ramp and one of our Piper Warriors, including a preflight and cockpit walkthrough with an instructor.

3

Two Intro Flights

Purchase the $199.95 Introductory Course: two one-hour lessons, a textbook, a logbook, and two ground school nights.

4

Start Training

Decide to continue and begin the four stages toward your Private Pilot certificate at your own pace.

Ready to stop dreaming and start flying?

Call the flight line or request a callback and a senior instructor will reach out.
