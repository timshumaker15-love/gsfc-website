The Introductory Course

A real evaluation of flight training, not just a joyride

Almost every flight school offers some kind of introductory flight. At Golden State we call ours an "Introductory Course" because it gives a prospective pilot a thorough experience for evaluating what's involved in earning a pilot certificate.

It starts with a no-cost, 2.5-hour Orientation Class where we walk through everything you'd want to know about learning to fly — whether here or anywhere else. We cover cost, time, and effort so you can make an informed decision. Next, you're escorted onto the ramp and shown around one of our Piper Warriors, including a preflight and cockpit checkout.

Qualified participants can then purchase the $199.95 Introductory Course, which includes two one-hour flying lessons, a textbook, a pilot logbook, and your choice of two nights sitting in on our Private Pilot Ground School. We schedule two hours of total time for each one-hour lesson to allow for pre- and post-flight requirements.

Fun and Rewarding

General Aviation pilots log more than 24 million hours a year — a passionate group who fly above traffic to places like Palm Springs, Temecula, Los Angeles, and cities across California's 217 general aviation airports. Call ahead to your destination's flying club and a rental car (or an Uber) is often waiting.

You Can Make Money, Too

Becoming a Private Pilot is the first stop toward endorsements, type ratings, and flight hours. Many students earn their Private Pilot license, become a Certified Flight Instructor, and teach other students while building hours at no cost — while earning more ratings themselves. A four-year degree isn't required; a background in meteorology, aviation, or aeronautical engineering (San Diego State University is nearby) is a nice addition to a resume, not a prerequisite.
