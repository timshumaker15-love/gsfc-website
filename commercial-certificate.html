Commercial Certificate

The certificate that lets you get paid to fly — what it lets you do, and what it costs at Golden State.

A Commercial Certificate is what allows you to be compensated for flying — carrying passengers or cargo for hire, banner towing, aerial photography, or building toward an airline career. It's the certificate that turns flying from a hobby into a paycheck.

Training builds on your Private Pilot and Instrument Rating, sharpening your precision flying to a higher standard and adding commercial-specific maneuvers, all typically flown in one of our Technically Advanced Aircraft. Most students move on from here toward a Certified Flight Instructor rating, the most common way pilots build hours toward an airline career — see Pilot Careers for what that path has looked like for past Golden State students.

At a Glance

  • Requires an Instrument Rating first
  • Flown in our Technically Advanced Aircraft
  • Must meet FAA Part 61 total, PIC, and cross-country time requirements
  • Separate FAA Knowledge Test and checkride
