Estimated Cost for Your Commercial Certificate

A typical cost breakdown for earning a Commercial Certificate at Golden State, assuming you already hold an Instrument Rating.

ItemHours / BasisEstimated Cost
Technically Advanced Aircraft (TAA)15 hrs @ $155$2,325
Instructor Hours30 hrs @ $80$2,400
Ground School (online)—$300
Books / Supplies—$200
Knowledge Exam—$175
Aircraft for Flight Test1.5 hrs @ $155$230
Examiner's Fee—$1,000
Total (estimated)$6,630
Assumes you already hold an Instrument Rating. You must also meet FAA Part 61 requirements for pilot-in-command time, cross-country time, and total time by the end of the course. Costs can be paid by the hour or at a discount for block time; actual cost will vary depending on hours needed. Flying regularly helps reduce hours required. Fuel surcharges may apply depending on variation in avgas costs.
