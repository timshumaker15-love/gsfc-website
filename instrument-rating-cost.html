A typical cost breakdown for earning your Instrument Rating at Golden State — pay by the hour, or save with block time.

Aircraft50 hrs @ $155$7,750
CFII Flight Instruction50 hrs @ $80$4,000
CFII Ground Instruction30 hrs @ $80$2,400
Ground School (online)—$300
Books / Supplies—$200
Knowledge Exam—$175
Aircraft for Flight Test1.5 hrs @ $155$232
Examiner's Fee—$1,000
Total (estimated)$16,057
Costs can be paid by the hour, or at a discount for block time. Actual cost will be higher or lower depending on hours needed — flying regularly helps reduce total hours. Fuel surcharges may apply depending on variation in avgas costs.
