FAA Airman Knowledge Testing

Golden State is an FAA-authorized testing center for a wide range of airman knowledge exams.

FAA Airman Knowledge Testing is available for online scheduling. If you're a new user, create an account below to get started (you'll need your FAA Tracking Number from IACRA.faa.gov).

Schedule Your Exam at faa.psiexams.com
Please read the registration requirements carefully — in particular, the requirements for identification and a current residential address. Per FAA/PSI testing procedures, if you arrive for an appointment without correct identification, or more than 30 minutes late, you will not be able to test and will forfeit the examination fee. Questions? Call us at (619) 449-0611.
