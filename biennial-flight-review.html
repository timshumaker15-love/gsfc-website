Biennial Flight Review: 4-Hour Class — $15

Held about four times a year, this refresher class helps you rediscover what you learned for your checkride but may have forgotten over time.

Our BFR classes run about four times a year, 9 AM to 1 PM. In four hours, you'll refresh the knowledge you built preparing for your checkride but may not have used since — helping you fly as a more confident, safer pilot.

Tuition is $15 and includes lunch. Call or come in to reserve your seat, or sign up using the form below. You're welcome to pay online in advance or simply pay when you attend.
